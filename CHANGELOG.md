# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-18)

First public release.

- Shopify and WordPress / WooCommerce websites.
- Categories, products (up to 8 price options, galleries, colour options, add-ons), pages, suburb pages, blog posts and 301 redirects.
- Review sheet before anything is loaded; nothing is added to the new site until the florist agrees.
- Resumes after an interruption; changes an already loaded site in place; checks every product, page and redirect afterwards.
- Tested on full florist websites: a WooCommerce site with 63 products, 16 pages and 230 blog posts, and a Shopify store with 97 products and 200 suburb pages.
